Privacy Policy for SUMO SCUFFLE

Effective date: 2026-11-14
Developer: Oskar Wetterbrandt
Contact: revolvermenteam@gmail.com

The short version. SUMO SCUFFLE does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no tracking. The game does not talk to the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few small files on your own device, in the app's private storage:

  • your settings (volume, controls, look options),
  • your progress (which levels, characters and outfits you have unlocked, and your match records).

These files never leave your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them.

Playing over your local network

SUMO SCUFFLE can play a match between two devices on the same Wi-Fi (or one phone's hotspot): "Host Game" on one device, "Find Game" on the other. This is the only networking in the game, and it works like this:

  • It only starts when you open Host Game or Find Game.
  • Devices talk directly to each other on your local network. There is no server and no connection to the internet.
  • While hosting, the game announces itself on the local network. The announcement contains a short room code, the game version, the name of your device (the phone model, for example "Pixel 7", or the computer name; shortened to 24 characters) and whether a match is running. During a match the devices exchange the game controls (joystick directions) needed to play.
  • Other devices on the same network that are running the game can see that announcement. Nothing is stored by the game afterwards.

This is why the Android app asks for the Internet, Network state, Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast permissions. They are used only for the local game above. If you only play on one phone or against the computer, no network traffic is sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising or an advertising ID.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • We do not share data with third parties, because we have none to share.
  • The game does not ask for or use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

Children

SUMO SCUFFLE collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete.

Third-party services

The game contains no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or accounts. It is built with the Godot Engine (free and open source), which does not send data anywhere on its own. Google Play itself may collect data about installing the game according to Google's own privacy policy; that is outside the game.

Changes

If the game ever changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, adding online play), this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
